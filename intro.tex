\subsection{PI Qualifications}

The team has made significant contributions to the fields of (embedded) systems and software security~\cite{zhang2019icore, zhao2011automatic, zhao2013using, tang2017nivanalyzer, jing2014morpheus, jing2014riskmon, jing2015towards, li2022understanding, ma2023dac, zhao24tee, wang24insect, byotee24}.
\textbf{PI Zhao}, an Associate Professor at the Khoury College of Computer Sciences at Northeastern University, has focused extensively on embedded system security.
His research group has been actively involved in systematically studying the security issues on MCUs.
For instance, they analyzed the hardware and architectural limitations of MCU systems and their ability to support the implementation of modern security solutions~\cite{tan24sok}.
Additionally, they crawled thousands of real-world MCU firmware samples to identify and analyze the security vulnerabilities present in them~\cite{nino24firware}.
Most relevant to \sysname, the team has a sustained track record at the microarchitectural hardware--software boundary --- the same layer at which glitching operates.
Supported by an NSF CRII award on defeating cache side-channel attacks, PI Zhao designed CacheLight~\cite{gutierrez2018cachelight} and SmokeBomb~\cite{cho2020smokebomb} to mitigate cache side-channel attacks on the ARM architecture, and uncovered the Prime+Count cross-world covert channel on ARM TrustZone~\cite{cho2018prime}.
Like fault injection, these attacks exploit physical and microarchitectural behavior rather than software bugs, giving the team direct experience with the threat model, measurement, and defenses this proposal targets.
